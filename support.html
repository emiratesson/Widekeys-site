Turn on the keyboard

  • Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  • Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose WideKeys
  • In any app, hold the 🌐 key and pick WideKeys

Do I need "Allow Full Access"?

No. WideKeys works without it. Turning it on adds haptics, key sounds, the clipboard, and lets the keyboard use the themes and settings you choose in the WideKeys app. WideKeys has no internet code, so nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

Add or remove a language

Open the WideKeys app → Settings → Languages → Add language. Swipe across the space bar to switch between your languages.

Change the look

Open the WideKeys app → Themes. Pick a style, its colours, key size and tap effect, then tap Save.

Report a problem

In the WideKeys app, go to Settings → Diagnostics → Report a problem. You can see the whole report before sending it; it never contains anything you typed.

Contact

Email widekeys.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
