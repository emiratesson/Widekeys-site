Turn on the keyboard

  • Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  • Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose WideKeys
  • In any app, hold the 🌐 key and pick WideKeys. To make it stick, put WideKeys at the top of the Keyboards list

Do I need "Allow Full Access"?

No. WideKeys works without it. Turning it on adds haptics, key sounds, the clipboard, and lets the keyboard use the look and settings you choose in the WideKeys app. WideKeys has no internet code, so nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

Why does the iPhone's own keyboard sometimes appear?

iOS always uses its own keyboard for password boxes, phone-number pads and in apps that don't allow keyboard apps. Right after a password box it sometimes keeps its own keyboard for the next box too: hold the 🌐 key and pick WideKeys to bring it back.

Keyboard size

Out of the box the keys are the same size, spacing and position as your iPhone's own keyboard; the number row is the one addition. To change the size, use Resize in the keyboard's ⚙︎ menu (drag the handles, or Reset), or the sliders in the app's Appearance tab. Reset to default keyboard size brings back your iPhone's sizes.

Add or remove a language

Open the WideKeys app → Settings → Languages → Add language. English, Arabic, Polish, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Turkish are included, with swipe typing and autocorrect; each language's own letters sit on their plain key (hold e for é). Swipe across the space bar to switch between your languages.

Change the look

Open the WideKeys app → Appearance. Choose Follow iPhone appearance, Light or Dark, a Pastel or Solid colour set, the keys' colours, roundness, letter size and where the small symbols sit on the keys, then tap Save. Follow iPhone appearance goes by your iPhone's own Light or Dark setting, not the app you're typing in, so the keyboard always matches the bar iOS draws under it.

Clipboard

Copied text is kept in the keyboard's clipboard panel until you remove it; choose a time limit instead in the app's Settings → Clipboard. Pin anything you want to keep.

User guide

The WideKeys app has a full, searchable guide: open the app and tap User guide.

Report a problem

In the WideKeys app, go to Settings → Diagnostics → Report a problem. You can see the whole report before sending it; it never contains anything you typed.
