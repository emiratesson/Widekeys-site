WideKeys is a keyboard for iPhone. We built it so that what you type stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. WideKeys does not collect, store, share, sell or send anything you type, and it has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The app and the keyboard contain no networking code.

What is saved on your iPhone

To work, WideKeys keeps a few things on your device only:

  • your theme and keyboard settings
  • your quick phrases
  • recently used emoji
  • clipboard history (text you copied or pasted using WideKeys)
  • words and short word pairs you type (for example "see you"), counted per language, so the keyboard can suggest your next word and improve swiping and autocorrect. Nothing is learned in password, email or web-address fields, and you can clear it at any time in Settings → Learning

These never leave your iPhone. Deleting WideKeys removes them. You can clear clipboard history at any time from the keyboard's clipboard panel.

Allow Full Access

iOS asks you to "Allow Full Access" before any keyboard can use haptics, key sounds, the clipboard, or share settings with its app. WideKeys uses Full Access only for those features. It does not use it to send data anywhere — the keyboard has no internet code. WideKeys still works if you leave Full Access off; only those extras are unavailable.

Diagnostics and problem reports

If iOS ever closes the keyboard unexpectedly, WideKeys notes on your iPhone what the keyboard was doing (for example "typing" or "switching language"), how long it had been open, how many keys were pressed (a count only), its memory use and your keyboard settings. This never includes anything you typed, learned words or your clipboard.

These notes stay on your iPhone unless you choose to send them: Settings → Diagnostics → Report a problem shows the complete report before you send it, and you send it yourself by email or another app of your choice. If you email us, we receive the report and your email address, use them only to fix the problem, and delete them once it's resolved. You can clear diagnostics at any time.

Spell-checking and suggestions

Autocorrect and word suggestions run entirely on your device, using Apple's built-in spell-checker and word lists included in the app. If you undo an autocorrection, that word is remembered by iOS on your device so it isn't corrected again.

Children

WideKeys collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
